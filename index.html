Upload the anonymized PDF of your submission. PaperScorer reads its pages and emails you the model's accept or reject decision, together with a calibrated confidence in this prediction.

How it works
  1. 1Upload the anonymized PDF. Only the first 15 pages are read; longer files are trimmed in your browser before upload.
  2. 2PaperScorer reads the page images with the model for your conference: PaperScorer-7B-Vision-OpenReview-ICLR for ICLR, and PaperScorer-7B-Vision-arXiv for every other venue.
  3. 3The decision and its confidence arrive by email.

Good to know

  • The result is the model's accept or reject decision plus a calibrated confidence in this prediction, not a review.
  • Before scoring, the worker re-checks the pages and the anonymization. If either fails, you get an email explaining what to fix.
  • Only the first 15 pages are read, and the PDF can be at most 20 MB.
  • Each email can submit up to 5 papers per day (UTC).

Examples of good papers

Below are well-known, good papers, anonymized and scored using PaperScorer-Vision. Each one is placed by its chance of acceptance divided by its venue's base rate (ICLR accepts ~31%, top venues accept ~25%), so 2× means twice as likely as a typical submission. Papers at 1× or above lean Accept. Two of these papers were actually rejected. The first is an early version of A Decade's Battle on Dataset Bias (ECCV 2024), which PaperScorer-Vision correctly predicts as a reject. The second is Mamba, rejected at ICLR 2024 but given a 71% chance (2.3× the ICLR base rate); many would argue it deserved acceptance. We also include the newer, accepted ICLR version of A Decade's Battle on Dataset Bias, which PaperScorer-Vision correctly predicts as an accept. These papers show that PaperScorer agrees with, and sometimes defies, previous decisions, based on its estimation of quality. Click a paper to see its pages.
