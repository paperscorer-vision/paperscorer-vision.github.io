How much to trust that chance

The chart checks PaperScorer's chances against the natural-rate test set: held-out papers the model never saw in training, at their real acceptance rate. They are grouped by the chance the model gave them. The solid line shows how many in each group were actually accepted; a perfectly calibrated model would sit on the dashed diagonal. The dotted line is the base rate: PaperScorer leans Accept for papers at or to the right of it.

    The chance of acceptance is calibrated with temperature scaling and a correction to the natural acceptance rate, both fit on a natural-rate validation split. PaperScorer reads only the first 15 pages.
